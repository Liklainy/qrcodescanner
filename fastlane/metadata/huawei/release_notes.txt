First release.

- Scan QR codes and barcodes (QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN, UPC, Code 128/39/93, ITF, Codabar) with the camera or from an image in the gallery.
- Flashlight and pinch-to-zoom in the scanner.
- Smart actions: call a number, add a contact or calendar event, write an email or SMS, show a location on a map, search for a product, add a Wi-Fi network.
- Create a QR code from any text and save or share it.
- Start a scan from the Quick Settings tile.
- Works offline; the camera is the only permission.
