QRefka Scan reads QR codes and barcodes and knows what to do with them: call a
number, save a contact, add an event to your calendar, join a Wi-Fi network or find
a product. It also turns text into a QR code you can save or share. Everything works
offline, and nothing you scan or create leaves your phone.

SCAN WITH THE CAMERA
- Point the camera at a code and the result appears right away.
- Flashlight button for dark places.
- Pinch to zoom for small or distant codes; double-tap to jump to 2x.

SCAN FROM AN IMAGE
- Pick a screenshot or a photo from your gallery and read the code in it.
- Works even if you have not given camera access.

SUPPORTED CODES
- 2D codes: QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417.
- Barcodes: EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF, Codabar.

SMART ACTIONS FOR EVERY RESULT
- Links: open in your browser or app.
- Phone numbers: call, or save as a new contact.
- Contact cards (vCard, MECARD): add to your contacts with name, phones, emails,
  company, address and website filled in.
- Calendar events: add to your calendar with the date, time and place filled in.
- Email: write a message with the address, subject and text already filled in.
- Text messages: open your messaging app with the number and text ready to send.
- Locations: show the place on a map.
- Product barcodes: search the web for the product.
- Wi-Fi: see the network name and security type and add the network in one tap,
  without typing the password.
- Any result can be copied to the clipboard.

CREATE QR CODES
- Type or paste any text or link and get a QR code.
- Save it to your gallery or share it with any app.

QUICK SETTINGS TILE
- Add the scanner to your Quick Settings panel and start a scan from anywhere.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- The camera is the only permission the app asks for. Calls, contacts, calendar
  events and Wi-Fi networks are saved by the system apps after you confirm, so the
  app needs no access to them.
- No internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
- No Google services required.
- Light and dark themes; English, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
- Open source under the MIT licence.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
