QR Scanner reads QR codes with your camera and turns text into a QR code you can
save or share. It works completely offline: nothing you scan or create leaves your
phone.

SCAN
- Point the camera at a code and the result appears right away.
- Open links, email addresses or map locations with one tap.
- Copy the result to the clipboard.
- Wi-Fi codes are recognised: the app shows the network name and security type and
  hands the credentials to the system "add network" dialog, so it never needs a
  Wi-Fi permission of its own.

CREATE
- Type or paste any text and get a QR code.
- Save it to your gallery or share it with any app.

QUICK SETTINGS TILE
- Add the scanner to your Quick Settings panel and start a scan from anywhere.

PRIVACY AND SIZE
- The camera permission is the only one the app asks for, and it is requested when
  you open the Scan tab.
- No internet permission at all, so no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
- Scan history is not stored.
- About 2.4 MB installed, with no Google services required.
- Open source under the MIT licence.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
